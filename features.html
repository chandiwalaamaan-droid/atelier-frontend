Features

Everything that gets checked

No black box — here's exactly what the analyzer looks at, and why each check matters.

01 · SECTIONS

Section detection

Confirms your resume has clearly labeled Experience, Education, and Skills sections — both recruiters and ATS parsers look for these by name, not just the content underneath.

02 · CONTACT

Contact info validation

Checks for a real email, phone number, and LinkedIn profile — missing contact details are one of the most common reasons a strong candidate never gets called.

03 · BULLETS

Action verbs & measurable impact

Flags bullet points that don't open with a strong action verb, and ones with no number or measurable result — the difference between "responsible for" and "reduced load time by 40%."

04 · CLICHÉS

Generic phrase detection

Catches filler like "hard worker" and "team player" — phrases that take up space without telling a recruiter anything specific about you.

05 · FORMAT

ATS formatting analysis

Inspects the actual PDF — not just the text — for embedded images, tables, and multi-column or sidebar layouts that often get scrambled when an ATS parses them out of order.

06 · MATCH

Job description matching

Paste a job posting and see your keyword match percentage instantly. Premium members get a deeper AI pass that tells the difference between skills you've actually proven and ones you've only name-dropped — plus rewrites of your weakest bullets for that exact job.

07 · UPLOAD

PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT & RTF support

Upload whatever format your resume is already in — no need to convert it first. Corrupted or scanned files get a clear explanation instead of a confusing error.

08 · READABILITY

Readability score

A Flesch reading-ease score and estimated grade level, so you know if your sentences are easy to skim or too dense for a 6-second recruiter scan.

09 · LEVEL

Career level detection

Automatically detects whether your resume reads as entry-level, mid, senior, or management, based on titles and years of experience mentioned.

10 · AI PREMIUM

AI suggestions & summary

Specific, personalized coaching — not generic checklist advice — plus a professional summary generated strictly from what's actually on your resume.

11 · REWRITE PREMIUM

Bullet point rewrites

Weak bullets rewritten with stronger verbs and clearer impact. If there's no real metric to work with, it inserts a placeholder like [X%] instead of making one up.

12 · COVER LETTER PREMIUM

AI cover letter generator

Paste a job posting and get a tailored cover letter in seconds — grounded only in your real experience, never invented achievements.

13 · INTERVIEW PREP PREMIUM

AI interview question prep

6–8 likely interview questions tailored to your resume and target role, each with concrete talking points you can actually use.

14 · GRAMMAR PREMIUM

Grammar & tone checker

Flags grammar, tense, and punctuation issues by quoting the exact phrase, plus an assessment of your resume's overall tone and consistency.

15 · LINKEDIN PREMIUM

LinkedIn summary generator

A ready-to-paste LinkedIn headline and About section, written in first person from your real resume content.

16 · EXPORT PREMIUM

Branded PDF report

A clean, shareable PDF of your full report — score, checklist, AI feedback, cover letter and interview prep all in one document.

17 · PROGRESS PREMIUM

Full score history & trend chart

Track your ATS score across every version of your resume, so you can see improvement over time instead of a one-off snapshot.

18 · UI

Dark mode

Toggle between light and dark themes from the nav bar on any page — your preference is remembered on your next visit.

19 · TRACKER

Application tracker

Log every company, role, salary, and recruiter contact in one place — with interview dates, follow-up reminders, and a status dashboard, instead of a scattered spreadsheet.

20 · COMPANY PREMIUM

Company match

See an estimated fit score for a specific company, your matching strengths, and the concrete gaps to close before you apply.

21 · ROADMAP PREMIUM

Skill roadmap

A concrete learning path from where your resume shows you are now to where your target role needs you to be.

22 · PRACTICE PREMIUM

Interview answer scoring

Type a practice answer to any prep question and get it scored — with specific improvements and the real resume details you forgot to mention.

23 · GITHUB PREMIUM

GitHub portfolio analyzer

See your public GitHub profile the way a recruiter would — top languages, stale or undocumented repos, and an honest recruiter-impression score.

24 · ROAST PREMIUM

Resume roast

Blunt, no-sugarcoating feedback on what's actually weak — for when you want the hard truth instead of another gentle suggestion.
